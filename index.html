My 5 Favorite Videogames of all Time

5. Risk of Rain 2

An absolute whirlwind of fun, Risk of Rain 2 is a fast-paced roguelike with a stunning soundtrack and an addictive gameplay loop.

4. Chivalry 2

Easy to pick up but difficult to master, Chivalry 2 has quick and engaging gameplay that makes it perfect for both casual and competetive players.

3. Elden Ring

While the difficult bosses and steep learning curve can make Elden Ring hard to get into, it's beautiful world and satisfying progression make it hard not to love.

2. Baldurs Gate 3

Whether its played with friends or on your own, Baldurs Gate 3 always seems to have something new to explore, and its story never fails to grip player's attention.

1. Minecraft

Despite playing Minecraft all my life I always seem to come back to it, and it always delivers a memorable experience.
